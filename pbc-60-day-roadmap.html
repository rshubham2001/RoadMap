How to use this plan

The schedule is intentionally sustainable around a full-time workday.

Baseline: about 15–16 focused hours/week — 90 minutes on weekdays and about 4 hours/day on weekends.

DSA: continuous practice using Striver A2Z as the backbone, plus LeetCode/pattern-wise practice and weekly contests.

System design: learn concept → attempt design → explain aloud → review → redesign.

Agentic AI: concept → code → small implementation. Do not attempt to finish every overlapping course end-to-end.

Interview habit: for every problem/design, practice explaining the reasoning, trade-offs, complexity and failure modes rather than only consuming videos.
